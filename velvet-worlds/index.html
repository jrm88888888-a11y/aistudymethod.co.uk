Your particles

Each world hides a fixed number. Once you have found them all, open another world.

Atom Lab

Build atoms from your particles. Protons decide which element it is.

The Forge

Bond atoms into a compound to make its weapon. A weapon you make works in every world.

How to open more worlds

There will be one world for each element. You open a world by owning its element, and you buy elements with Velvet coins from mini-lessons and the arcade.
