The game formats

Each topic runs through a whole set of formats, so you're testing the same content in lots of ways — which is exactly what makes it stick:

Quiz

Rapid recall on definitions, formulae, ion charges and reaction types.

Memory match

Pair elements with symbols, or ions with their charges.

Sorting

Group ideas — metals vs non-metals, exothermic vs endothermic, acids vs bases.

Word games

Hangman, anagram and crossword play on key chemistry vocabulary.

Higher/lower & sequence

Rank by reactivity or atomic number, or order the steps of a method.

Arcade games

Pac-style and space-invaders-style games with chemistry questions built in.

How it fits your revision

Games are the easiest way to test yourself — low effort to start, and they quickly show which topics are solid and which aren't. When a game keeps catching you out — an ionic formula you can't balance, a definition of an isotope that won't stick, the mole equation you keep fumbling — that's your signal to go and understand it properly, then come back and prove it's fixed.

If you want the depth to go with the drilling, the Velvet Method and our courses show you how to use AI to explain a tricky mechanism, quiz you and mark your working — the games stay free either way.
