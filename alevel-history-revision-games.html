The game formats

The Arcade has 22 game formats, so you meet the same ideas in lots of different ways — recognising them, recalling them and using them under time pressure:

14 question games

Spot the lie, find the odd one out, stake your chips, race a ghost — built from the topic's key ideas.

Two Truths, One Lie · Odd One Out · Connections Grid · Sort the Sequence · Wager · Higher or Lower · Falling Words · Conveyor Belt · Boss Rush · Ghost Race · The Claw · Coin Pusher · Word Web · Reveal Race

8 key-term games

Match, spell, unscramble and shoot down the topic's key terms and definitions.

Vocab Quiz · Matching Pairs · System Breach · Term Guess · Anagram · Crossword · Pac-Man Vocab · Space Invaders

A few formats need a particular kind of content, so they open only where the topic has it — Sort the Sequence needs a true order, and Higher or Lower needs real numbers.

How it fits your revision

Games are the lowest-effort way to test yourself, and testing — not re-reading — is what shows you what you really know. A few rounds on a topic quickly show which parts are solid and which have gaps. When a game keeps catching you out on the same term, that's your signal to go back and learn it properly, then return and prove it's fixed.

For the depth to go with the drilling, the free A-Level History revision guide covers the key concepts and common mistakes topic by topic, and the Velvet Method and our courses show you how to use AI to explain a tricky idea, quiz you and mark your answers — the games stay free either way.
