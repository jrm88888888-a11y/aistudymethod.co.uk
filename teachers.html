Two ways to use it

One lesson, or a whole session.

Assign a single lesson

Every mini-lesson has a Teachers: assign this button.

  1. Find the lesson in your course list below.
  2. Press Assign.
  3. Choose Microsoft Teams, Google Classroom, a link, or a QR code for the board.

Run a class session

One link takes students through up to three steps on the same topic.

Quiz→Mini-lesson→Games

The quiz shows what they already know, the lesson teaches it, the games practise it. Games exist for some topics so far; the session uses the steps available for that topic.

Common questions

Questions teachers ask us.

Do students need an account?
No. Students open the link and start. Every lesson, quiz and game works without signing up.
Do students need ChatGPT or another AI tool?
No. The mini-lessons, confidence quizzes and revision games do not use an AI chatbot, so they work on school networks that block AI tools.
Can I see my students' scores?
Not yet. Students see their own score at the end of each lesson. If class results would help you, tell us below.
Do the lessons follow my exam board?
Each course page lists the lessons for one exam board in the order of that board's specification topics. KS3 follows the National Curriculum programme of study, and the IB Diploma follows the IB subject guide.
