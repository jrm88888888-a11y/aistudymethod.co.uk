Why no-login matters: most "free" revision sites make you create an account, hand over an email, or sit through a paywall before you can play. Here there's nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay and no email to give — just click and play. That's the whole point.

What you get

  • 18 game formats — one topic, lots of different ways to test yourself.
  • Every subject and topic across GCSE, plus A-Level and IB.
  • Works on any device — phone, tablet or laptop, straight in the browser.
  • Retrieval practice, not passive re-reading — you're pulling answers out of your own head, which is what actually builds memory.

The games are the low-friction way in. You don't have to psych yourself up for an hour of revision — you just open one, play for a few minutes, and you've tested your recall on a real topic without noticing the effort.

The game formats

You'll find quizzes, word games, sorting challenges, timelines and proper arcade-style games — all pulling from your chosen subject and topic.

Quiz

Games are the easiest way to test recall — they turn revision into something you'll actually pick up. But testing is only half the job. When a game shows up a gap, that's your cue to go deeper: understand the idea properly, then come back and prove it stuck.
