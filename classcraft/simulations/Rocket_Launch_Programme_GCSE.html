ROCKET LAUNCH PROGRAMME

Mathematics • GCSE

10:00
SETUP

How to Play

Rocket Launch Programme is a 7-round simulation where teams make strategic decisions across 6 key metrics.

Each Round has 4 phases:

  • Briefing — Read the situation report and any random events aloud.
  • Decision Cards — Show the 5 decisions. Teams do the calculations on paper or mini-whiteboards, then discuss (~10 min, use the timer).
  • Team Entry — Each team taps A, B, or C for each decision.
  • Results — Reveal the worked solutions step by step, compare them with each team's working, then show the score changes.

Metrics: Altitude Reached, Fuel Efficiency, Budget Remaining, Mission Success Probability, Crew Safety, Research Level. Each decision shifts these metrics. The team with the best overall performance wins.

Scoring: Final score is the sum of all metric values (normalised to their scales).
