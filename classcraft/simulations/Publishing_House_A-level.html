PUBLISHING HOUSE

English • A-level

10:00
SETUP

How to Play

Publishing House is a 7-round simulation where teams make strategic decisions across 7 key metrics.

Each Round has 4 phases:

  • Briefing — Read the situation report and any random events aloud.
  • Decision Cards — Show the 5 decisions. Teams discuss (~10 min, use the timer).
  • Team Entry — Each team taps A, B, or C for each decision.
  • Results — Scores update and metric changes are displayed.

Metrics: Sales Revenue, Critical Acclaim, Diversity Index, Cultural Influence, Author Satisfaction, Profit Margin, Backlist Value. Each decision shifts these metrics. The team with the best overall performance wins.

Scoring: Final score is the sum of all metric values (normalised to their scales).
