How Paper 2 works

Paper 2 lasts 1 hour 30 minutes. You answer two essay questions, each from a different world history topic, each marked out of 15 (total 30). It is the same paper for SL and HL.

Comparative thinking

Why comparison is the key skill

Each topic is thematic and comparative: questions ask you to analyse an idea (causes, methods, effects) across examples from different regions. A response resting on a single country rarely reaches the top bands.

Region rule

Draw on more than one region

Wherever a question allows, use examples from at least two regions — for instance comparing authoritarian rule in Germany and China, or 20th-century wars in Europe and Asia. This demonstrates genuine world-historical range.

Argument

Thesis and structure

Open with a clear thesis that answers the exact question. Structure thematically, not chronologically, so each paragraph develops an analytical point supported by specific, dated evidence, and build toward a reasoned judgement.

Skill

Historiography

Using historians

Strong answers show awareness of historiography — how different historians have interpreted the past — and weigh those interpretations rather than merely quoting them. Even a brief, accurate reference to a school of thought lifts an essay.

Command terms

Reading the question

Watch the command term: "Evaluate", "To what extent", "Compare and contrast", "Examine". Each demands analysis and a judgement — never just description. Underline it and answer it directly.

Exam craft

Timing and planning

Split your time evenly: roughly 40-45 minutes per essay, with a quick plan first. A short plan that fixes your thesis and three thematic points prevents drift and keeps both essays balanced.

Recap

The big ideas to take away

Format: 1h30, two 15-mark essays from two of twelve topics (SL and HL alike)

Topics: twelve thematic topics from 750 CE to the 20th century

Key skill: compare across regions, not within one country

Argument: thesis, thematic structure, dated evidence, judgement

Extra marks: engage historiography and answer the command term directly
