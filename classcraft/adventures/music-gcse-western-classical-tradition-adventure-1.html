Baroque Sounds

Bach, Vivaldi and the harpsichord

The Baroque era (c.1600–1750) features ornate melodies, terraced dynamics (sudden loud/soft) and a continuous basso continuo. Keyboard instrument: the harpsichord.

Key composers: J.S. Bach (fugues, Brandenburg Concertos), Vivaldi (Four Seasons), Handel (Messiah).

Classical Clarity

Haydn, Mozart, Beethoven

The Classical era (c.1750–1820) values balance, clear phrases and gradual crescendos. The piano replaces the harpsichord. Sonata form dominates first movements.

Listen for Mozart's elegant melodies, Haydn's witty surprises, and Beethoven bridging into the Romantic era with bigger emotion.

Romantic Drama

Bigger emotion, bigger orchestra

The Romantic era (c.1820–1910) explores intense emotion, expanded orchestras and rich chromatic harmony. Programme music tells stories — like Berlioz's Symphonie Fantastique.

Key composers: Schubert (lieder), Chopin (piano), Brahms, Wagner (operas with leitmotifs).
