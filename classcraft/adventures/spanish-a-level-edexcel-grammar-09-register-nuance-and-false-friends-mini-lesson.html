Register, nuance & false friends

A-Level examiners reward precision. This lesson sharpens four things that separate a good answer from a great one: por vs para, the ser/estar adjectives that change meaning, the difference between formal and informal register, and the amigos falsos — words that look English but aren't.

por vs para · para

PARA = goal, purpose, destination

Use para when you point forward — to a goal, a recipient, a destination or a deadline:

  • Goal / purpose: Estudio para aprobar. — I study (in order) to pass.
  • Recipient: Es para ti. — It's for you.
  • Destination: Salgo para Madrid. — I'm leaving for Madrid.
  • Deadline: Para el lunes. — By / for Monday.

Memory hook: para looks ahead — to the aim, the person it's for, or the deadline.

por vs para · por

POR = cause, exchange, duration, movement through

Use por when you look back to a cause, or express exchange, duration or motion through:

  • Cause / reason: Gracias por el regalo. — Thanks for the gift.
  • Exchange / price: Pagué 10€ por el libro. — I paid 10€ for the book.
  • Duration: Por dos años. — For two years.
  • Movement through: Viajo por España. — I travel through Spain.

Rule of thumb: para = the aim (why you do it); por = the cause, cost, time or route.

ser vs estar · meaning shift

Adjectives that change meaning

Some adjectives mean one thing with ser (permanent trait) and another with estar (state):

adjectivewith serwith estar
aburridoboringbored
listocleverready
  • Es aburrido. — He is boring. (his character)
  • Está aburrido. — He is bored. (right now)
  • Es listo. — He is clever. / Está listo. — He is ready.

Trait vs state: ser gives the essential quality; estar gives the passing condition.

Register · tú vs usted

Formal and informal "you"

Spanish chooses the pronoun by register. The catch for A-Level: usted (formal) takes the él/ella verb form, not a "you" form:

registersingularplural
informaltú (tienes)vosotros (tenéis)
formalusted (tiene)ustedes (tienen)

The trap: usted tiene — not usted tienes. Formal "you" borrows the third-person form (tiene, habla, es).

Recap

Your nuance toolkit

por = cause (gracias por), exchange (pagué por), duration (por dos años), through (por España).

para = goal (para aprobar), recipient (para ti), destination, deadline (para el lunes).

ser/estar shift: ser aburrido (boring) / estar aburrido (bored); ser listo (clever) / estar listo (ready).

Register: usted takes the él/ella form (usted tiene); ustedes for plural formal.

False friends: éxito = success, actual = current, sensible = sensitive, embarazada = pregnant, realizar = to carry out, largo = long.
