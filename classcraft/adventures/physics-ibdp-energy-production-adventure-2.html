Stop 1

Blackbody radiation

Every object emits EM radiation depending on T.

P = σ A T⁴ (Stefan-Boltzmann).
σ = 5.67 × 10⁻⁸ W/(m²K⁴).

λ_max T = b (Wien), b = 2.9 × 10⁻³ m·K.
Sun (5800 K): λ_max ~500 nm — visible green-yellow.

Stop 2

Earth's balance

Solar constant S = 1361 W/m².

Power intercepted: S × πR² (disk).
Re-emitted from sphere: σ × 4πR² × T⁴.

Albedo α ≈ 0.30 → 30% reflected.
Equilibrium: S(1-α)/4 = σT⁴ → T ≈ 255 K (-18°C).

Actual surface: +15°C — difference of 33 K from greenhouse effect.

Stop 3

Enhanced greenhouse

Greenhouse gases (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, H₂O) absorb IR re-emitted by Earth and re-radiate downward.

CO₂ rose from 280 ppm (pre-industrial) to ~420 ppm (2023). T up ~1.1°C since 1900 (IPCC AR6).

Effects: sea level rise, extreme weather, ocean acidification, species shifts.

Paris Agreement (2015): keep warming < 2°C, ideally 1.5°C — politically + scientifically global.
