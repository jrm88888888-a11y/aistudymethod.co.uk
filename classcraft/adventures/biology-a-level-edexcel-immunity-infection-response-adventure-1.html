Immunity, Infection & Response
Now in 1 mini-lesson.

This topic is taught in the mini-lesson below. Each mission starts from a question: make a guess, learn the idea, then check it. About 20 minutes each. No login.

Topic 6: Immunity, Infection and Forensics (A2) (part 1 of 2)

  1. A body is found with no witnesses. What clues inside and on it reveal how long ago the person died?
  2. How can a speck of DNA from one hair be copied into billions and then sorted by size?
  3. If almost all human DNA is shared, how can a trace of it point to just one person?
  4. How does one virus turn the body's own defenders into factories that make more of it?
  5. Your body carries trillions of microbes, yet very few make you ill. What makes something a pathogen?
  6. Most germs that land on you never get in, and most that do never get far. What stops them?
