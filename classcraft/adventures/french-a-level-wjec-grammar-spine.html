The lessons in order

Every WJEC A-Level French mini-lesson in the order to work through it. Grammar drives the order: each lesson builds on the one before, then the theme lessons put it to work on the topics of the course, and the skills lessons prepare the exam tasks.

Each lesson opens with a question, then works through it in short missions with checks. About 20 minutes per mission.
