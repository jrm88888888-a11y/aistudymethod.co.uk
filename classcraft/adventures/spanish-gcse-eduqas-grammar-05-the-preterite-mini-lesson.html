The preterite

The preterite is the simple past — the tense for completed actions at a specific, known time. It answers "what happened?": ayer fui a la playa (yesterday I went to the beach). This lesson builds it: regular -ar/-er/-ir endings, the ser / ir / dar irregulars, the "pretérito grave" stem verbs, and the yo-form spelling changes.

Work through each screen and answer the questions as you go — some ask you to recognise the right form, some ask you to produce it by typing. Collect ⭐ stars and press Start.

What the preterite does

Completed, at a known time

Use the preterite for a past action that started and finished — a single event, or a series of events, seen as done. It usually comes with a time marker that pins it to a moment:

  • ayer — yesterday
  • anoche — last night
  • la semana pasada — last week
  • el año pasado — last year
  • el verano pasado — last summer
  • hace dos días — two days ago

What it is not: the preterite is not for describing or for "used to". A separate past tense (the imperfect) does that — you'll meet it in cluster 6. For now: if it happened once and finished, use the preterite.

Regular verbs · Step 1

-AR verbs: the biggest family

Take the infinitive hablar (to speak), chop off -ar, and add the preterite endings. Watch the accents on yo and él:

hablar — to speak (spoke)
personform
yohablé
túhablaste
él / ella / ustedhabló
nosotroshablamos
vosotroshablasteis
ellos / ustedeshablaron

Two things to notice: the accents fall on hablé ("I spoke") and habló ("he/she spoke") — drop them and you change the word. And hablamos looks identical to the present ("we speak" / "we spoke") — context decides which.

ser, ir and dar

ser and ir are IDENTICAL in the preterite — fui, fuiste, fue… Context tells you which: Fui a Madrid = "I went to Madrid"; Fue difícil = "It was difficult". dar borrows the -er/-ir endings but takes no accents: di, dio (not dí, dió).

The "pretérito grave" group

Irregular-stem verbs

A big group of common verbs takes a changed stem and one special set of endings — the same for all three verb types, and crucially with no accents:

new stem + -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieronno accent on the "yo" or "él" forms — that's the giveaway
tener → stem tuv- (to have → had)
personform
yotuve
tútuviste
él / ella / ustedtuvo
nosotrostuvimos
vosotrostuvisteis
ellos / ustedestuvieron

Learn the stems — the endings above never change:

  • tener → tuv- (tuve…)  ·  estar → estuv- (estuve…)
  • poder → pud- (pude…)  ·  poner → pus- (puse…)
  • querer → quis- (quise…)  ·  venir → vin- (vine…)
  • hacer → hic- (hice, hiciste, hizo, hicimos, hicisteis, hicieron) — c→z in the "él" form so it stays "th/s"‑soft
  • decir → dij- (dije, dijiste, dijo, dijimos, dijisteis, dijeron) — the "ellos" ending is -jeron, no i
  • traer → traj- (traje… trajeron) — same -jeron quirk

Two spellings to burn in: hacer gives hizo ("he did/made"), not hició; and decir gives dijeron ("they said"), not dijieron.

The "yo" spelling swaps

Some regular -ar verbs change spelling in the yo form only, to keep the sound of the infinitive. Everything else in the table is completely regular:

ending → yoexample
-car → -québuscar → busqué
-gar → -guéllegar → llegué  ·  jugar → jugué  ·  pagar → pagué
-zar → -céempezar → empecé  ·  cruzar → crucé

Only "yo" changes. The rest stay regular: llegaste, llegó, llegamos, llegasteis, llegaron. These carry extra credit at Higher, but even at Foundation they're worth knowing — llegar and empezar are everywhere.

Recap

Your preterite toolkit

What it's for: completed actions at a specific/known time — "what happened". Markers: ayer, anoche, la semana pasada, el año pasado, hace dos días.

Regular -ar: -é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -asteis, -aron (accents on hablé / habló).

Regular -er / -ir (same endings): -í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -isteis, -ieron.

ser = ir: fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron. dar: di, dio… (no accents).

Pretérito grave: new stem + -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron — tuve, estuve, pude, puse, quise, vine, hice (hizo), dije (dijeron).

Yo spelling swaps: buscar→busqué, llegar→llegué, empezar→empecé.

3rd-person -ir swaps: pidió, durmió, prefirió (and their -ieron).
