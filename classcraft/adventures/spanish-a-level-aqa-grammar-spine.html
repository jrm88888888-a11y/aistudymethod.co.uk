The grammar backbone

Ten interactive mini-lessons that build and consolidate the whole grammatical backbone of AQA A-Level Spanish — from the tense system to the subjunctive, si-clauses and the passive. GCSE grammar is assumed; this takes it to A-Level depth. Work through them in order.

1 · The teaching

Ten grammar mini-lessons — the graspable overview of the grammar you must command.

2 · The practice fuel

Authentic vocabulary and set-text language ride on top as drills and games.

Scope: this is the grammar pillar of A-Level. It does not replace your set text & film, essay technique, translation practice or the Individual Research Project — it makes the grammar underneath them secure.
