Mission 1

One stem, two sets of endings

  • English adds a word: "will", "would". French changes the ending.
  • Stem = the infinitive (drop the final -e of -re verbs): parler-, finir-, vendr-.
  • Future = stem + -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont.
  • Conditional = same stem + imperfect endings -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient.

Mission 1

What the conditional does

  • Would: À ta place, je refuserais. – In your place, I'd refuse.
  • Politeness: Je voudrais un renseignement. – I'd like some information. · Pourriez-vous m'aider ? – Could you help me?
  • Advice with devoir: Tu devrais en parler à tes parents. – You should talk to your parents about it.
  • Cautious opinion: Il serait utile de limiter le temps d'écran. – It would be useful to limit screen time.
  • Unconfirmed news (press style): Selon la police, le suspect serait en fuite. – According to the police, the suspect is reportedly on the run.
  • Future in the past: Il a promis qu'il viendrait. – He promised he would come.
  • Watch out: not every "would" is a conditional.
    • "would" = used to → imperfect: Le dimanche, nous allions chez ma grand-mère. – On Sundays we would go to my grandmother's.
    • "would not" = refused → vouloir: Elle n'a pas voulu répondre. – She wouldn't answer.

Mission 1

After quand and dès que: future, not present

  • English: future in the main clause, present after "when". "When I am 18, I'll vote."
  • French is logical: if the action is in the future, both verbs are future.
  • Quand j'aurai dix-huit ans, je voterai. – When I'm 18, I'll vote.
  • Dès que les résultats seront publiés, nous vous contacterons. – As soon as the results are published, we'll contact you.
  • Same rule after lorsque (when), aussitôt que (as soon as), tant que (as long as):
    • Tant qu'il y aura des inégalités, il y aura des tensions sociales. – As long as there are inequalities, there will be social tensions.
  • Main clause an imperative? Still future: Appelle-moi quand tu arriveras. – Call me when you arrive.
  • Watch out: quand + present is right for habits and general truths: Quand il pleut, je prends le bus. – When it rains, I take the bus.
  • Watch out: si is not on this list: Si tu viens, je serai content. (present after si; see a later lesson)
  • Your turn: "As soon as I have a job, I'll buy a car."

Mission 1

The same logic in the past

  • Reporting a promise from the past? Future → conditional in both clauses.
  • « Je t'appellerai dès que j'arriverai. » → Il a promis qu'il m'appellerait dès qu'il arriverait.
    • He promised he would call me as soon as he arrived.
  • Elle savait qu'elle partirait quand elle aurait assez d'argent. – She knew she would leave when she had enough money.
  • Finished first? Dès que j'aurai fini… is the future perfect.
  • Use it: projecting the future of a theme (technology, environment) in the speaking discussion.

Mission 2

Future perfect: "will have done"

  • Future of avoir / être + past participle.
  • j'aurai fini · tu auras compris · elle sera partie · nous nous serons installé(e)s · ils auront reçu
  • Same auxiliary and agreement rules as the passé composé.
  • Use: an action finished before a point in the future.
    • D'ici la fin de l'année, j'aurai obtenu mon permis. – By the end of the year, I'll have got my licence.
    • Dans dix ans, l'intelligence artificielle aura transformé le monde du travail. – In ten years, AI will have transformed the world of work.
  • Also: a guess about the past. Il n'est pas là ? Il aura oublié. – He's not here? He must have forgotten.

Mission 2

Finished first: quand + future perfect

  • Mission 1: after quand, lorsque, dès que, a future action takes the future.
  • If that action is finished before the main one: future perfect.
  • English uses "have done". French doesn't.
    • Quand tu auras fini tes devoirs, tu pourras sortir. – When you have finished your homework, you can go out.
    • Dès que nous serons arrivés, nous vous appellerons. – As soon as we've arrived, we'll call you.
    • Une fois que les élèves auront voté, on annoncera les résultats. – Once the students have voted, the results will be announced.
  • après que works the same way: Après qu'il aura parlé, nous voterons. – After he has spoken, we'll vote.
  • Watch out: après que takes the indicative, not the subjunctive.
  • Your turn: "As soon as I've read the article, I'll send it to you."

    Dès que j'aurai lu l'article, je te l'enverrai.

  • Conditional of avoir / être + past participle.

  • Regret: J'aurais aimé étudier la musique. – I would have liked to study music.
  • After si + pluperfect: Si j'avais su, je serais venu. – If I'd known, I would have come.
  • Unconfirmed past news: L'incendie aurait fait trois blessés. – The fire reportedly injured three people.
Mission 2

Should have, could have

  • devoir and pouvoir in the conditional perfect + infinitive:
    • j'aurais dû + infinitive – I should have…
    • j'aurais pu + infinitive – I could have / might have…
    • il aurait fallu + infinitive – we / they should have… (it would have been necessary)
  • On aurait dû agir plus tôt contre le réchauffement climatique. – We should have acted sooner against global warming.
  • Les autorités auraient pu éviter la crise. – The authorities could have avoided the crisis.
  • Il aurait fallu investir dans les transports publics. – They should have invested in public transport.
  • Watch out: after dû / pu, an infinitive: j'aurais dû fait → j'aurais dû faire.
  • Watch out: je devrais = I should (now). J'aurais dû = I should have.
  • Use it: evaluating past policy in the speaking discussion or essay.

Recap

The big ideas

Mission 1

  • Future and conditional share one stem (infinitive or irregular -r- stem); future endings -ai…-ont, conditional -ais…-aient.
  • Conditional = would, politeness, advice, unconfirmed news, future in the past. "Would" = used to → imperfect.
  • After quand, lorsque, dès que, aussitôt que, tant que: future for a future action (conditional in the past). After si: present.

Mission 2

  • Future perfect = future of avoir/être + participle: done before a future point; after quand, dès que, une fois que, après que for the action finished first.
  • Conditional perfect = conditional of avoir/être + participle: would have; regret; unconfirmed past news.
  • j'aurais dû / pu + infinitive = should have / could have; il aurait fallu + infinitive.
