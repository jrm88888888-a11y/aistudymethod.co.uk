Connectives · Contrast

Discourse markers of contrast

To set one idea against another — the backbone of a balanced argument:

  • cependant / néanmoins / pourtant — however / nevertheless
  • en revanche — on the other hand
  • malgré — in spite of · bien que — although (+ subjunctive)

Example: Cependant, cette idée a ses limites. — However, this idea has its limits.

Addition & consequence

Building & concluding an argument

Two more families you must deploy:

  • Addition: de plus / en outre / par ailleurs — moreover / furthermore / besides
  • Consequence: donc / par conséquent / ainsi — so / therefore / thus
  • Structure: d'une part… d'autre part — on one hand… on the other; tout d'abord — first of all; en conclusion / pour conclure — in conclusion

Exam gold: opening with tout d'abord and closing with en conclusion gives your essay a clear, marked structure.

Opinion

Opinions & the subjunctive

How you introduce an opinion decides the mood of the verb that follows:

  • je pense que + indicative — I think that… (asserted as fact)
  • je ne pense pas que ce soit… — I don't think it is… (subjunctive, doubt)
  • il est possible que… — it is possible that… (subjunctive, possibility)

The flip: affirmative je pense que takes the indicative; negate it (je ne pense pas que) and it takes the subjunctive — a reliable way to show IB French B range.

Translation technique

Don't translate word-for-word

Translation marks are won and lost on detail. The core rules:

  • Watch false friends: éventuellement = possibly, not "eventually".
  • Check gender agreement and adjective endings.
  • Mind word order: most adjectives follow the noun in French.
  • Rework structure: don't map English word-by-word onto French.

Classic example: "I have been learning French for 3 years" = j'apprends le français depuis trois ans — present tense + depuis, not a perfect tense.

Recap

Your essay & translation toolkit

Contrast: cependant, néanmoins, pourtant, en revanche, malgré, bien que.

Addition & consequence: de plus, en outre, par ailleurs; donc, par conséquent, ainsi. Structure with d'une part… d'autre part, tout d'abord, en conclusion.

Opinion: je pense que + indicative; je ne pense pas que ce soit / il est possible que + subjunctive.

Translation: avoid word-for-word; watch faux amis (éventuellement = possibly); "for 3 years" = depuis trois ans + present.
