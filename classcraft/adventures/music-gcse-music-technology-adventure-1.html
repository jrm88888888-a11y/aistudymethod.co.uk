What MIDI Actually Is

Data, not sound

MIDI doesn't carry audio — it carries instructions: which note, when it started, how hard (velocity), and when it stopped. Your DAW or synth then plays those instructions on any sound.

Because MIDI is data, you can easily change a piano part to a violin, transpose it, or fix wrong notes after recording.

Inside a DAW

Logic, Ableton, GarageBand, FL Studio

A DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) is where you record, arrange, edit and mix music. Popular ones: Logic Pro, Ableton Live, GarageBand, FL Studio, Cubase.

All DAWs share core features: a timeline, tracks (audio and MIDI), a mixer, and a piano roll for editing MIDI notes.

Loops & Quantise

Building tracks block by block

A loop is a short section (often 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars) that repeats. Modern producers build whole tracks by layering loops in different combinations across sections.

Quantise snaps slightly mistimed notes to a beat grid (e.g. 1/16). Use it lightly — too much quantising can make your performance sound robotic.
