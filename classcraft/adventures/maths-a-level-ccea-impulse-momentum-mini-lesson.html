Mechanics · impulse and momentum (A2 only)

Momentum and impulse

  • Momentum =mv=mv. It is a vector: in one dimension, choose a positive direction and give velocities signs. Units: N s (or kg m s⁻¹).
  • Impulse of a constant force FF acting for time tt is FtFt, in N s.
  • Impulse = change in momentum:
I=mv−muI=mv−mu
  • The impulse has the same direction as the change in velocity.

Question
A ball of mass 0.2 kg hits a vertical wall at 15 m s⁻¹ and rebounds along the same line at 10 m s⁻¹. Find the impulse on the ball, and the average force if contact lasts 0.04 s.

Mechanics · impulse and momentum (A2 only)

Conservation of linear momentum

  • When two bodies collide, they exert equal and opposite impulses on each other (Newton's third law).
  • So, with no external impulse, total momentum before = total momentum after:
m1​u1​+m2​u2​=m1​v1​+m2​v2​m₁u₁+m₂u₂=m₁v₁+m₂v₂
  • Coalesce: the bodies stick and share one velocity, (m1​+m2​)v(m₁+m₂)v.
  • Explosion from rest: total momentum is zero before, so the parts move in opposite directions.
Worked example Mechanics · impulse and momentum (A2 only)

A head-on collision

Question
Particle AA (3 kg) moving at 5 m s⁻¹ collides directly with particle BB (2 kg) moving at 4 m s⁻¹ towards AA. After the collision AA moves at 1 m s⁻¹ in its original direction. Find the velocity of BB after the collision.
  1. 1
    Take AA's original direction as positive, so BB's velocity is −4−4
  2. 2
    Momentum before: 3(5)+2(−4)=73(5)+2(−4)=7
  3. 3
    Momentum after: 3(1)+2vB​=73(1)+2v_B=7, so vB​=2v_B=2
  4. 4
    Check impulses: on AA, 3(1−5)=−123(1−5)=−12; on BB, 2(2−(−4))=122(2−(−4))=12
Answer
BB moves at 2 m s⁻¹ in AA's original direction (it reverses)
⚠️ Watch out
writing BB's initial velocity as +4+4 gives vB​=10v_B=10, which is wrong.

Mission check

?A 1 kg trolley at 4 m s⁻¹ hits a stationary 3 kg trolley and they stick together. How fast do they move?

Total momentum before is 1×4=41 × 4=4 N s, and after the collision the combined 4 kg must carry the same momentum: 4v=44v=4, so v=1v=1 m s⁻¹. Kinetic energy falls from 8 J to 2 J; it is momentum, not energy, that is conserved in the collision.

Recap

Big ideas

  • Momentum =mv=mv (a vector); impulse =Ft=mv−mu=Ft=mv−mu, in N s.
  • With no external impulse, total momentum is conserved in collisions, coalescence and explosions.
  • Choose a positive direction first and give every velocity its sign.
