Mission 1

One tense, three English meanings

  • English uses different forms to describe the past:
    • used to (a habit): I used to have a dog.
    • was / were + -ing (going on): I was watching TV.
    • was + description: It was great.
  • French uses one tense for all three: the imperfect.
  • Quand j'étais petit, j'avais un chien. – When I was little, I had a dog.
  • Je regardais la télé. – I was watching TV.
  • C'était génial. – It was great.
  • Think of it as the background: habits, scenes, how things were.
  • Your turn: what does J'avais un vélo rouge mean?
    Show answer

    I had (used to have) a red bike.

Mission 1

Four phrases to use every time

  • These four make any past story richer:
FrenchEnglishExample
c'étaitit wasC'était génial. – It was great.
il y avaitthere was / there wereIl y avait beaucoup de gens. – There were lots of people.
j'étaisI wasJ'étais content(e). – I was happy.
il faisait + weatherit was + weatherIl faisait chaud. – It was hot.
  • Add them to a perfect-tense story: Samedi, je suis allé(e) à la plage. Il faisait beau et c'était génial.
  • Watch out: il y avait means "there was" and "there were". It never changes.
  • Watch out: weather uses faire. Il était chaud means "He was hot". Say Il faisait chaud.
  • Your turn: "It was boring and there were lots of tourists."
    Show answer

    C'était ennuyeux et il y avait beaucoup de touristes.

Build it: I, you, he, she

  • Three steps:
    • Take the nous form of the present: nous jouons.
    • Remove -ons: jou-.
    • Add the endings -ais, -ais, -ait.

  • Before a vowel: je → j'. j'avais, j'habitais.

Mission 1

Spelling and sound traps

  • -ger verbs keep the e before a: manger → je mangeais.
  • -cer verbs take ç before a: commencer → je commençais.
  • These keep the soft sound, just like nous mangeons, nous commençons.
  • je parlais, il parlait, ils parlaient all sound the same.
  • Watch out: "used to" is not utiliser. J'utilisais jouer → Je jouais.
  • Watch out: "was watching" is one word, not étais + verb. J'étais regarder → Je regardais.
  • Your turn: which is right, je mangais or je mangeais?
    Show answer

    je mangeais

Mission 2

Dots and lines

  • A past story has two layers.
  • Perfect = the events. Single, finished actions. Dots on a timeline.
  • Imperfect = the background. Description, feelings, weather, habits. A line under the dots.
  • Samedi, je suis allé(e) à la plage. – On Saturday I went to the beach. (dot)
  • Il faisait beau. – The weather was nice. (line)
  • J'ai mangé une glace. – I ate an ice cream. (dot)
  • C'était génial. – It was great. (line)
  • Your turn: dot or line? Il y avait beaucoup de gens.
    Show answer

    Line: it describes the scene, so imperfect.

Mission 2

Three questions to choose

  • Ask:
    • Did it happen once and finish? → perfect.
    • Is it a description (weather, feelings, what it was like)? → imperfect.
    • Did it happen regularly ("used to")? → imperfect.
  • Time words help:
Usually perfectUsually imperfect
hier – yesterdaytous les jours – every day
samedi dernier – last Saturdaysouvent – often
un jour – one dayquand j'étais petit(e) – when I was little
ensuite, puis, enfin – then, next, finallyle samedi – on Saturdays
  • Watch out: samedi = on Saturday (once). le samedi = on Saturdays (every week).
  • Your turn: "Yesterday I played football. It was cold."
    Show answer

    Hier, j'ai joué au foot. Il faisait froid.

Mission 2

Was doing … when …

  • A long action was going on (imperfect) when a short event happened (perfect).
  • Je regardais la télé quand mon frère est arrivé. – I was watching TV when my brother arrived.
  • Il faisait beau quand nous sommes partis. – The weather was nice when we left.
  • J'étais au parc quand j'ai vu Léa. – I was in the park when I saw Léa.
  • Line first, then the dot cuts across it.
  • Watch out: English often uses "-ing" for the line. That "-ing" is your signal for the imperfect.
  • Your turn: what does Je dormais quand ma mère est rentrée mean?
    Show answer

    I was sleeping when my mother came home.

Mission 2

Same English, different French

  • "I went" (once) → je suis allé(e). "I used to go" → j'allais.
  • "I played" (once) → j'ai joué. "I was playing" → je jouais.
  • "It was" (description) → c'était. Not il a été for a description.
  • "I had a dog" (for years) → J'avais un chien. "I had a pizza" (once) → J'ai pris une pizza.
  • Watch out: "used to" is never utiliser. J'utilisais jouer → je jouais.
  • Your turn: what is the difference? Je jouais au foot / J'ai joué au foot
    Show answer

    I used to play (was playing) football / I played football (once).

Recap

The big ideas

Mission 1

  • Imperfect = used to, was -ing, was + description.
  • c'était, il y avait, j'étais, il faisait are your four go-to phrases.
  • Stem: nous form minus -ons (être → ét-). Endings -ais, -ais, -ait, then -ions, -iez, -aient.

Mission 2

  • Perfect = single, finished events: je suis allé(e), j'ai perdu.
  • Imperfect = background: description, weather, feelings, habits: c'était, il faisait, il y avait, j'étais.
  • "Was -ing … when …": imperfect line, perfect dot.
