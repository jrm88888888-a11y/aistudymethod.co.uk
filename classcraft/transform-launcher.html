Transform

Transform is the final step: look back, then plan. Work out what is secure now, what still isn't, and which way of studying worked — then decide what to come back to, and when.

Reflect and plan with AI

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini at the end of a session. It asks you three questions, then turns your answers into a plan for the next two weeks.
