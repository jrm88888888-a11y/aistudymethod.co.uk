## gcse-es-blocks-2
- concern: Lesson [18] (all 7 copies) says «una gran ciudad» = "a great city (not a big one)". That is too absolute: before nouns for places and things, «gran» often still means big or large (una gran ciudad is commonly "a big city"); the clean great/big contrast holds for people. The check therefore uses people (una gran mujer / un chico grande). The hook narration asks the question without asserting the answer, so it is not wrong, but Billy may want [18] softened to "often means great".
- aqa-grammar-01-building-blocks: [18] "great, not big" is over-absolute for things and places (see concerns).
- verify: why: limited the "great" shift to people, since «gran» before things and places often still means big (e.g. un gran edificio).
