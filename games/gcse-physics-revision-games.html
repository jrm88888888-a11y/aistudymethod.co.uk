Free GCSE Physics Revision Games — No Login, No Sign-Up

Drill every GCSE Physics topic — energy, forces, waves, electricity and the rest — through 22 quick game formats. No account, no email, no download. Just pick a topic and play.

Why games work so well for Physics: physics is half concepts and half recall — the equations, units, definitions and required-practical details you simply have to know cold. Games turn that memorisation into two-minute bursts of retrieval practice, which is far better for memory than re-reading your notes.

Pick a topic and play

Every GCSE Physics topic has its own set of games pulling the key terms, quantities and definitions for that unit:

The game formats

Each topic runs through 18 different formats, so you're testing the same content in lots of ways — which is exactly what makes it stick:

Quiz

Rapid recall on definitions, units and equations for the topic.

Memory match

Pair quantities with their units, or terms with definitions.

Sorting

Group ideas — e.g. scalars vs vectors, or renewable vs non-renewable.

Word games

Hangman, anagram and crossword play on key physics vocabulary.

Higher/lower & sequence

Order quantities by size or steps of a process.

Arcade games

Pac-style and space-invaders-style games with physics questions built in.

How it fits your revision

Games are the easiest way to test yourself — low effort to start, and they quickly show which topics are solid and which aren't. When a game exposes a gap (a definition you keep missing, an equation that won't stick), that's your signal to go and understand it properly, then come back and prove it's fixed.

If you want the depth to go with the drilling, the Velvet Method and our courses show you how to use AI to explain a tricky idea, quiz you and mark your working — the games stay free either way.
