Free GCSE Biology Revision Games — No Login, No Sign-Up

Biology is the most vocabulary-heavy science — cell structures, enzymes, body systems and processes you have to name precisely. Turn all that memorising into quick retrieval-practice bursts across 22 game formats. No account, no email, no download.

Play Biology games → All subjects

Why games suit Biology especially well: more than any other science, Biology rewards precise recall — the exact word for an organelle, the steps of a process, the definition an examiner is looking for. Games break that memory load into two-minute rounds of retrieval practice, which fixes terminology far more firmly than reading it over again, and they're perfect for the many labelled-diagram and keyword-dense topics.

Every GCSE Biology topic has its own set of games pulling the key terms, definitions and processes for that unit — from naming cell parts to working out food-chain relationships:

The game formats

Each topic runs through 18 different formats, so you meet the same terms and processes in lots of ways — which is exactly what makes biology vocabulary stick:

Quiz

Rapid recall on definitions, structures and processes for the topic.

Memory match

Pair organelles with their functions, or terms with definitions.

Sorting

Group ideas — e.g. plant vs animal cells, or aerobic vs anaerobic respiration.

Word games

Hangman, anagram and crossword play on key biology vocabulary.

Sequence & higher/lower

Order the stages of a process, or rank quantities by size.

Arcade games

Pac-style and space-invaders-style games with biology questions built in.

How it fits your revision

Games are the lowest-effort way to test yourself — and with so much biology terminology to hold, testing is exactly what you need. A quick round quickly shows which topics are solid and which have gaps. When a game keeps catching you out on the same enzyme or the same step of a process, that's your signal to go and learn it properly, then come back and prove it's fixed.

If you want the depth to go with the drilling, the Velvet Method and our courses show you how to use AI to explain a tricky process, quiz you on key terms and mark your working — the games stay free either way.

Questions students ask

Yes — completely free, with no account, no email and no download. Every Biology game is free to play across all topics.

All of them — cell biology, organisation, infection and response, bioenergetics, homeostasis and response, inheritance, variation and evolution, and ecology. You pick a topic and the games pull its key terms, definitions and processes.

Does it cover my exam board (AQA, Edexcel, OCR)?

Yes. The core GCSE Biology content — key definitions, processes and required-practical ideas — is shared across the main boards, and you can pick your board and topic in the Arcade.
