The game formats

Each topic runs through many different formats, so you're testing the same content in lots of ways — which is exactly what makes it stick:

Quiz

Rapid recall on definitions, units and key facts across all three sciences.

Memory match

Pair terms with definitions, or quantities with their units.

Sorting

Group ideas — e.g. elements vs compounds, or renewable vs non-renewable.

Word games

Hangman, anagram and crossword play on key science vocabulary.

Higher/lower & sequence

Order quantities by size, or steps of a process such as the cell cycle.

Arcade games

Pac-style and space-invaders-style games with science questions built in.

How it fits your revision

Games are the easiest way to test yourself across all three sciences at once — low effort to start, and they quickly show which topics are solid and which aren't. When a game exposes a gap, that's your signal to go and understand it properly, then come back and prove it's fixed.

Questions students ask

Are the GCSE Combined Science games free?

Yes — completely free, with no account, no email and no download. Every Combined Science game is free to play across biology, chemistry and physics.

Which GCSE Combined Science topics are covered?

Every topic on the AQA Combined Science: Trilogy specification — all the biology, chemistry and physics content, with the separate-science-only material left out.

Is this Combined Science or the separate sciences?

This shelf is AQA Combined Science: Trilogy (8464) — the version most GCSE students take, worth two GCSEs. If you sit the three separate sciences, use the individual Biology, Chemistry and Physics shelves instead.

Do the Combined Science games work on a phone?

Yes. They run in any browser on phones, tablets and laptops — nothing to install.
