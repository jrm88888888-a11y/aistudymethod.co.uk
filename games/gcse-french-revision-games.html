The game formats

Each topic runs through 18 different formats, so you're testing the same content in lots of ways — which is exactly what makes it stick:

Quiz

Rapid recall on vocabulary and verb forms for the theme.

Memory match

Pair each word with its English meaning to lock the vocab in.

Sorting

Group words by gender, tense or theme.

Word games

Hangman, anagram and spelling play on the theme's core vocabulary.

Sequence

Order the steps of a process or a set of numbers/dates.

Arcade games

Pac-style and space-invaders-style games with vocab questions built in.

How it fits your revision

Games are the easiest way to test yourself — low effort to start, and they quickly show which topics are solid and which aren't. When a game exposes a gap, that's your signal to go and understand it properly, then come back and prove it's fixed.

Games build vocabulary and verb recall — the foundation — not speaking or writing fluency. Once the words are secure, you still need to practise producing them in speech and writing.

If you want the depth to go with the drilling, the Velvet Method and our courses show you how to use AI to explain a tricky idea and quiz you — the games stay free either way.

Questions students ask

Are the GCSE French games free?

Yes — completely free, with no account, no email and no download. Every game is free to play across all themes.

Which GCSE French topics are covered?

The core themes — identity and relationships, school, free time, healthy living, local area, travel and the environment — with games built around each theme's vocabulary and common verbs.

Will games make me fluent?

No — that's honest. They build the vocabulary and verb recall you need as a foundation. Speaking and writing fluency come from producing the language, which the Velvet Method can help you practise with AI.

Do they work on a phone?

Yes. They run in any browser on phones, tablets and laptops — nothing to install.
