AP® Chemistry: short lessons to assign, unit by unit

16 short, curiosity-driven AP Chemistry lessons in College Board course framework order, each labeled with its College Board unit and topic numbers. Use them alongside AP Classroom practice: they build the understanding behind the questions, one mission (about 20 minutes) at a time. No student login. Press Assign to choose what to set (quiz, lesson or both) and share it through Google Classroom, Teams, a link or a QR code.

How AP teachers use these lessons

  • Before a new topic: assign the mission that opens it, so students arrive with a question to answer.
  • Alongside AP Classroom: when a progress check shows a misconception, assign the lesson for that topic number.
  • Homework: students get instant feedback on every question. No login or account needed.
  • Absent students and sub plans: students work through a lesson on their own.
