Why games help with History: the exam runs on precise recall — dates, key figures, causes and consequences. Games turn that chronology and detail into fast retrieval practice, so you can back up an argument with accurate evidence.

The game formats

Each topic runs through 18 different formats, so you're testing the same content in lots of ways — which is exactly what makes it stick:

Quiz

Rapid recall on key terms, definitions and concepts for the topic.

Memory match

Pair terms with meanings, or ideas with the thinker/case behind them.

Sorting

Group ideas into categories — arguments, causes, viewpoints, features.

Word games

Hangman, anagram and crossword play on the topic's key vocabulary.

Sequence

Put events, stages or a chain of reasoning into the right order.

Arcade games

Pac-style and space-invaders-style games with questions built in.

How it fits your revision

Games are the easiest way to test yourself — low effort to start, and they quickly show which topics are solid and which aren't. When a game exposes a gap, that's your signal to go and understand it properly, then come back and prove it's fixed.

History options vary between schools, so not every unit here will be yours — play the ones you study. The games build accurate recall of dates, figures and events; source analysis and essay technique still need separate practice.

If you want the depth to go with the drilling, the Velvet Method and our courses show you how to use AI to explain a tricky idea and quiz you — the games stay free either way.

Questions students ask

Are the GCSE History games free?

Yes — completely free, with no account, no email and no download. Every game is free to play across all study units.

Which GCSE History units are covered?

A range of the most commonly-taught options — Elizabethan and Norman England, Germany 1890–1945, inter-war and Cold War conflict, America 1840–1895, and the Health and Power thematic studies. Pick the units your school teaches.

My exam board and options are different — will it still help?

History options differ a lot by school and board, so choose the units you actually study. The Arcade lets you pick your board and topic to match as closely as possible.

Do the History games work on a phone?

Yes. They run in any browser on phones, tablets and laptops — nothing to install.
