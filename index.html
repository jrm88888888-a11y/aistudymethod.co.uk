The system behind the lessons

Six steps. One system. Every lesson is step three.

The Velvet Method is the route through any course: see it whole, find your gaps, learn, verify, explore, transform. Mini-lessons are the Learn step, and the site now provides more of each step itself every week. Tap a letter to see what that step does.

In practice
STEP 01
V
View the Bigger Picture
Map your syllabus with AI before studying a single topic. Build the mental framework everything else attaches to.
STEP 02
E
Evaluate Your Starting Point
Run an AI diagnostic across all topics. Turn vague anxiety into a precise, actionable priority list.
STEP 03
L
Learn Creatively
Fill gaps using the format that engages you most — games, roleplay, analogies, Feynman technique.
STEP 04
V
Verify Your Knowledge
Retrieval practice with AI-generated exam questions. Prove knowledge is secure, not just familiar.
STEP 05
E
Explore Application
Apply and analyse in novel real-world contexts — the level that separates good grades from excellent ones.
STEP 06
T
Transform Through Reflection
Review the cycle, build metacognitive awareness, and plan the next session with clarity.
