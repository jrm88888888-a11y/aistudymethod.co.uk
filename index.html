The Methodology

Six steps. One system. Infinite subjects.

Grounded in schema theory, retrieval practice, Bloom's Taxonomy, and metacognitive research — the Velvet Method uses AI to make these principles accessible for every student.

STEP 01
V
View the Bigger Picture
Map your syllabus with AI before studying a single topic. Build the mental framework everything else attaches to.
STEP 02
E
Evaluate Your Starting Point
Run an AI diagnostic across all topics. Turn vague anxiety into a precise, actionable priority list.
STEP 03
L
Learn Creatively
Fill gaps using the format that engages you most — games, roleplay, analogies, Feynman technique.
STEP 04
V
Verify Your Knowledge
Retrieval practice with AI-generated exam questions. Prove knowledge is secure, not just familiar.
STEP 05
E
Explore Application
Apply and analyse in novel real-world contexts — the level that separates good grades from excellent ones.
STEP 06
T
Transform Through Reflection
Review the cycle, build metacognitive awareness, and plan the next session with clarity.

Why It Works

Built on real science.
Taught by real educators.

🧠

Schema Theory

The View step builds the mental framework before content is learned — new knowledge attaches more deeply to existing structure.

🔁

The Testing Effect

Retrieval practice in the Verify step is one of the most robust findings in cognitive science — far more effective than re-reading.

🎯

Bloom's Taxonomy

The Explore step targets higher-order thinking — application and analysis — which consistently differentiates grade boundaries.

🪞

Metacognitive Learning

John Hattie's research identifies self-regulation as one of the highest-impact educational interventions available.
