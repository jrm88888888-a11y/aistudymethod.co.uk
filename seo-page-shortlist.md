# SEO Page Shortlist — AI Study Method

*Grounded in a UK SERP recon, July 2026. Strategy: don't fight the content-volume war on head terms (PMT / Save My Exams / Seneca / Bitesize own those). Win the **AI-method + parent-anxiety** space and the **no-login games** angle, where page 1 is currently US sites, small tutors, forums, or thin aggregators — not the revision brands.*

**Two principles behind the list**
1. **Content is the hook; the product is the moat.** AI Overviews / ChatGPT will answer these questions inline, so a ranking alone isn't the win — each page must funnel into a thing an AI answer can't hand over: the Velvet Method, the £25 course, the prompt books, the games.
2. **These are trust queries (parents, near-YMYL).** Google rewards demonstrated expertise, so every AI/parent page needs a real author byline — Clare (Oxford/Durham) and James (teacher) — plus real social proof once the survey fills. The SEO work and the credibility work are the same work.

---

## Tier 1 — Flagship (build these first: 4 pages)

Highest winnable × on-strategy, all leveraging assets you already have, small enough for a two-person team.

**1. "How to stop your child cheating with AI — a teacher's honest guide"**
- Target: *how to stop my child cheating with ChatGPT / AI* (UK). **Winnability: HIGH** — page 1 is US/teacher-facing only; no UK *parent* answer exists. Your sharpest single term.
- Asset: for-parents positioning + founder credentials (E-E-A-T). Reframes the fear into "here's the legitimate way" → the Method/course.
- CTA: the £25 course ("the responsible-use system"); secondary: free prompt books.

**2. "Is my child cheating with AI? What UK parents need to know"**
- Target: *is my child cheating with AI* (GCSE / UK schools). **HIGH** — the term is entirely US-held right now.
- Include/adjacent: **"falsely accused of using AI"** — a rising, emotionally charged sub-topic nobody UK owns; superb trust-builder that positions your method as the legitimate path.
- Asset + CTA: same as #1.

**3. "How to use ChatGPT to revise for GCSE — the right way (dos and don'ts)"**
- Target: *how to use ChatGPT to revise for GCSE* + *ChatGPT dos and don'ts revision*. **HIGH** — page 1 is small tutors + a Student Room thread. This page literally *is* your method.
- Asset: the Velvet Method + prompt books. CTA: free prompt book for their subject → course.

**4. "Free GCSE revision games — no login, no sign-up" (indexable hub)**
- Target: *GCSE revision games no login / no sign up* (an uncontested phrase — nobody names it) + *free online revision games for GCSE*. **HIGH/uncontested** (a GitHub repo currently ranks — the SERP is weak).
- **This reconciles the recent noindex fix:** the ~1,800 game tool pages are now noindexed, so this is a NEW *indexable* landing page that links *into* them and owns the "no login" positioning.
- Honest note: low search volume — treat as a **conversion/differentiator** page, not a traffic driver.

## Tier 2 — Supporting AI/parent + per-subject games (scale after Tier 1)

**5. "Is using ChatGPT to do homework cheating? (UK schools / GCSE)"** — Target same. **MED–HIGH** with the UK/GCSE qualifier (bare term is US/forum-led).

**6. "How to revise with AI for GCSE (not university)"** — Target *how to revise using AI*. **MED–HIGH** — the only real competition is UK university study-skills pages; a GCSE-pitched angle is open.

**7. "AI as a tutor, not a shortcut — study smarter, not harder"** — the responsible-use framing your course sells; captures *use AI responsibly / smarter not harder* phrasings. Method + course CTA.

**8. Per-subject game hubs (×4 strongest subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths)** — e.g. *"Free GCSE Physics revision games — no login"*. **HIGH** — weak, aggregator SERP. Each indexable, links into that subject's noindexed games. Repeatable template.

**9. "Turn your revision notes into a podcast with AI (NotebookLM)"** — a specific, low-competition AI how-to that maps directly to the prompt-book product. Rising interest, almost no dedicated UK GCSE page.

## Tier 3 — Ultra-long-tail subject on-ramp (only where genuinely winnable; template + scale)

The recon confirms head/concept subject terms are a **brand wall** — you win **only at the ultra-specific level**, and best where the intent is *practice* (which suits your interactive games).

**10. Per-required-practical pages** — *"AQA GCSE Physics Required Practical [N]: [name] — method, results & common mistakes"* (and Biology). **MED** — mixed SERP (Cognito, PDFs, small sites rank RP-by-RP). Win one practical at a time; each ends with the matching game + an AI self-test prompt.

**11. "Practice"-intent interactive pages** — *"GCSE Chemistry mole calculations — practice (no login)"*. **MED–HIGH** — "practice" is served by small sites/worksheets, not a brand lock; a no-login interactive is the opening. Maps straight to a game.

**Do NOT target (confirmed walls):** *GCSE physics revision*, *energy stores explained*, *quadratic formula explained* (revision-brand-owned); *best AI tools for GCSE revision*, *AI study tips* (commercial-listicle / SaaS-owned).

## What every page needs (to rank AND convert)
- **UK + GCSE/board qualifiers** — the competition is mostly US or generic; the UK-GCSE angle is the wedge.
- **A real, credentialed author byline** (Clare / James) — non-negotiable for the trust queries.
- **A clear funnel** — end with the relevant product (course / prompt book / game), not just "read more".
- **Internal links** from the topic maps (the on-ramp) and between the AI/parent pages (topical authority cluster).

## Sequencing
Start with the **four Tier-1 pages** — highest winnability, all reuse existing assets, publishable quickly. Publish → get indexed → let Search Console accumulate impressions over ~4–8 weeks. *That* becomes the first-party data you don't have yet, and it tells you which Tier-2/3 bets to double down on.

## Honest caveats
- Parent-anxiety UK demand is **diffuse** (much of the visible activity is US) — you'll be *building* UK demand as much as capturing it. Right for a moat play, slower for quick traffic.
- "No login" is genuinely **low volume** — a differentiator/conversion asset, not a traffic engine.
- Tier 3 wins only at the **ultra-specific** grain; anything broader hits the wall.
- Accuracy note for any strategy doc: it's **18 games**, not 17 (corrected sitewide), and the current indexable footprint is ~330 pages (mostly the 310 revise guides), not ~199.
