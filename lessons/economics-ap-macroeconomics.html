11 free lessons, unit by unit.

Free AP Macroeconomics review and study guide, in College Board unit order, with the course framework topic numbers on every lesson: GDP, inflation and unemployment, AD-AS, money and the Fed, fiscal and monetary policy, and exchange rates. Short, curiosity-driven lessons that teachers can assign: each one opens with a question you can’t yet answer, then works through it in missions with AP-style checks and interactive graphs. About 20 minutes per mission. Use them alongside AP Classroom practice. No login needed.
