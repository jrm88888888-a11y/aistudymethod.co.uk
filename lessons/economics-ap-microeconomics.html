13 free lessons, unit by unit.

Free AP Microeconomics review and study guide, in College Board unit order, with the course framework topic numbers on every lesson: supply and demand, elasticity, costs and perfect competition, monopoly and oligopoly, factor markets and market failure. Short, curiosity-driven lessons that teachers can assign: each one opens with a question you can’t yet answer, then works through it in missions with AP-style checks and interactive graphs. About 20 minutes per mission. Use them alongside AP Classroom practice. No login needed.
