Research Methods

Experimental and qualitative methods, sampling, ethics and data analysis.

Research Methods is a core part of IB Psychology. Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

ExperimentManipulates an independent variable to measure an effect.
HypothesisA testable prediction.
VariablesIndependent, dependent and controlled variables.
SamplingSelecting a representative group from a population.
EthicsConsent, confidentiality and protection from harm.
Data analysisDescriptive and inferential statistics.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

Does a correlation prove that one thing causes another?✗ Yes, a correlation proves cause and effect.   ✓ No — correlation shows a relationship, not causation.
Why do psychologists use larger samples?✗ To make the effect bigger.   ✓ To improve reliability and how representative the findings are.
