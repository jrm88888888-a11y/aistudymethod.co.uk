GCSE German Revision

Grammar & Key Verbs

High-frequency verbs, tenses and core grammar you need across every topic.

Grammar & Key Verbs is a core part of GCSE German. Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

eins, zwei, dreiOne, two, three — the start of counting 1 to 20.
MontagMonday — first weekday; days start with capitals in German.
JanuarJanuary — months are also capitalised in German.
rotRed — colour adjective, no capital letter.
blauBlue — common GCSE colour adjective.
schwarzBlack — note the 'sch' sound and ending -z.
gutGood — also used to say 'well' (Mir geht es gut).
großBig or tall — opposite of klein.
kleinSmall or short — common GCSE descriptor.
schönBeautiful or nice — useful for places and people.
hässlichUgly — opposite of schön; useful contrast adjective.
altOld — also 'years old' with Ich bin. Jahre alt.
einundzwanzigTwenty-one — pattern: digit + und + tens.
hundertHundred — einhundert or just hundert.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

How many genders do German nouns have?✗ Two, like French and Spanish.   ✓ Three — masculine, feminine and neuter (der, die, das).
Is German word order the same as English?✗ Yes, exactly like English.   ✓ No — the verb is usually second in main clauses and last in subordinate clauses.
