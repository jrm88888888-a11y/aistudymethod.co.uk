External Influences

How the economy, law, technology, ethics and the environment affect business decisions.

External Influences is a core part of GCSE Business Studies. Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What are the four Ps of the marketing mix?✗ The four Ps are Product, Price, Profit, and People.   ✓ Product, Price, Place (distribution), and Promotion — the controllable variables a business uses to satisfy customers and achieve objectives.
What does break-even output represent?✗ Break-even is where the business makes the most profit.   ✓ The level of output where total revenue equals total costs — neither profit nor loss. It is NOT the level where profit is maximised; profit grows beyond break-even.
Do all businesses aim only to maximise profit?✗ Yes, profit is every business's only goal.   ✓ No — aims vary: survival, growth, market share, social aims.
Does a sole trader have limited liability?✗ Yes, sole traders have limited liability.   ✓ No — sole traders have unlimited liability for business debts.
What is the difference between profit and revenue?✗ Profit and revenue are different words for the same thing.   ✓ Revenue is total income from sales (price x quantity sold); profit is revenue minus total costs — what is left after paying for everything.
