Key concepts

Magnetic field, B⃗A vector field: at each point it has a magnitude and a direction, and it determines the magnetic force exerted on moving charges, currents and magnetic materials there.
Magnetic field of a moving charged particleA charged particle moving with velocity v⃗ produces a magnetic field in the space around it, in addition to its electric field.
Biot–Savart lawThe law giving the magnetic field dB⃗ produced at a point by a short current element I dℓ⃗: dB⃗ = (μ₀/4π)I(dℓ⃗ × r̂)/r², where r is the distance from the element to the…
Ampère’s lawFor steady currents, the line integral of the magnetic field around any closed path equals μ₀ times the net current through a surface bounded by that path: ∮B⃗ · dℓ⃗ =…
Magnetic dipoleA source of magnetic field with a north and a south polarity, such as a bar magnet, a compass needle, a current loop or a circulating charge.
Vacuum permeability, μ₀The constant that sets the strength of magnetic fields produced by moving charges and currents in empty space: μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A.
Current element, I dℓ⃗A short length dℓ of a current-carrying wire, taken as a vector in the direction of the conventional current, multiplied by the current I.
Line integral ∮B⃗ · dℓ⃗The sum of B⃗ · dℓ⃗ = B dℓ cos θ over every element of a closed path, where θ is the angle between the field and the path element.
Magnetic monopoleA hypothetical isolated north or south pole.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer. Make sure you know the right one:

Are a magnet's north and south poles the same thing as positive and negative electric charges?✗ A magnet's poles behave like electric charges: north acts like positive and south like negative, so a magnet attracts or repels charged objects at rest, and charge alone makes a magnetic pole.   ✓ No.
Is the field produced by a current element largest at points straight ahead of it, along the line of the current?✗ The field of a current element depends on the component of the element along the line to the point (dℓ cos θ), so it is largest straight ahead of the element and zero beside it.   ✓ No.
For an Amperian loop in the plane of the page traversed counterclockwise, which currents through the loop count as positive in I_enc?✗ For a counterclockwise Amperian loop in the plane of the page, currents into the page count as positive in I_enc.   ✓ Currents out of the page (⊙).
