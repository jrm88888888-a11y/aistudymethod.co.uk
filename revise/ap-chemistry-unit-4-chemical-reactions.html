Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer. Make sure you know the right one:

Do changes of phase of water make or break water molecules?✗ Changes of phase of water break or form the bonds within its molecules: boiling splits water into hydrogen and oxygen, and condensation forms water from hydrogen and oxygen in the air.   ✓ No.
When a reaction goes to completion, are all of the reactants used up?✗ When a reaction goes to completion, all of every reactant is converted into products, so no reactant is left over.   ✓ Not unless they are mixed in exactly the mole ratio of the balanced equation.
Is a redox equation balanced once the atoms of each element are equal on both sides?✗ A redox equation is balanced when the atoms of each element are equal on both sides; the total charge on each side need not be the same.   ✓ No.
