Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer. Make sure you know the right one:

In Φ_B = BA cos θ, is θ the angle between the magnetic field and the plane of the loop?✗ The angle θ in Φ_B = BA cos θ is measured between the magnetic field and the plane of the loop.   ✓ No.
Does an inductor oppose the current itself, acting like an extra resistance?✗ An inductor opposes the current itself, so it acts like an extra resistance and reduces even a steady current.   ✓ No.
When the current in an LC circuit is at its maximum, is the energy shared equally between the capacitor and the inductor?✗ At the instant of maximum current, the circuit's energy is shared equally between the capacitor and the inductor, so (1/2)LI_max² is half of Q₀²/(2C).   ✓ No.
