A-Level Geography Revision

Global Systems & Governance

Globalisation, trade and TNCs, global governance and the role of superpowers.

Global Systems & Governance is a core part of A-Level Geography (AQA, Edexcel, OCR and other boards). Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

GlobalisationIncreasing interconnection of economies, cultures, politics through trade, ICT, migration.
WTOWorld Trade Organization (164 members, 1995); reduces tariffs and resolves disputes.
IMFInternational Monetary Fund (190 members); structural adjustment loans, often criticised for conditionality.
World BankProvides development loans; HIPC initiative cancelled debt for 37 countries.
Trade blocGroup of countries with reduced internal trade barriers; EU, NAFTA/USMCA, ASEAN.
Uneven developmentGlobal core-periphery patterns; high-income vs low-income GDP per capita gap.
TNCTransnational corporation operating in multiple countries; Apple, Nike, Shell are examples.
Global production networkGeographically dispersed supply chain; Apple iPhone sources from 43 countries.
OutsourcingContracting tasks to external firms, often abroad (Foxconn for Apple).
OffshoringRelocating own operations to lower-cost countries.
GlocalisationAdapting global products to local markets; McDonald's McAloo Tikki in India.
ReshoringBringing manufacturing back; US firms partly reshoring from China post-2018.
Gini coefficientInequality measure (0 = perfect equality, 1 = max inequality).
HDIHuman Development Index combining income, education, life expectancy (0-1).

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

Why do TNCs locate production in LICs?✗ Because TNCs want to develop poorer countries and lift them out of poverty.   ✓ Lower labour costs, weaker regulation, access to raw materials, large potential markets, and government incentives like tax breaks in special economic zones.
Why can a cold winter NOT disprove climate change?✗ A really cold winter proves global warming is not happening.   ✓ Climate is the long-term statistical average; a single cold winter is a weather event. Climate change is the trend in averages and extremes over decades, not the absence of cold days.
What is the difference between a megacity and a world city?✗ Megacities and world cities are the same thing.   ✓ A megacity is defined by population (over 10 million); a world city is defined by global economic and cultural influence (e.g. New York, London) — so a city can be one without the other.
What is the difference between outsourcing and offshoring?✗ Outsourcing and offshoring are the same thing.   ✓ Outsourcing means contracting work to another company; offshoring means relocating work to another country. A firm can outsource without offshoring, or offshore without outsourcing (in-house overseas).
Why is HDI a better measure of development than GNI per capita?✗ Because HDI measures how happy people are.   ✓ HDI combines income, life expectancy and education, capturing quality of life — GNI per capita is just average income and hides inequality and non-economic wellbeing.
