Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What did Magna Carta actually do in 1215?✗ Magna Carta gave ordinary English people democratic rights and freedom.   ✓ It was a peace treaty between King John and rebel barons protecting baronial privileges and limiting arbitrary royal taxation and imprisonment — it was annulled within months.
Why did Chartism fail in the short term?✗ Chartism failed because no one supported it.   ✓ Mass petitions were rejected by Parliament; the movement split between moral-force and physical-force wings; economic improvement after 1848 reduced support.
What caused the Peasants' Revolt of 1381?✗ The peasants revolted because they were starving and had no food.   ✓ The poll tax of 1381 on top of long-term anger over labour laws restricting peasant wages after the Black Death and resentment at clerical wealth.
Who led the Peasants' Revolt of 1381?✗ Simon de Montfort   ✓ Wat Tyler
What did the 1832 Great Reform Act actually do?✗ The 1832 Reform Act gave the vote to all working men.   ✓ It abolished rotten boroughs, redistributed seats to industrial towns, and extended the vote to about 1 in 5 adult men — still far short of democracy.
