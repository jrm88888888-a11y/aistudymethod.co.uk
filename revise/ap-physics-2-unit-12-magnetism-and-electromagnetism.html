Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer. Make sure you know the right one:

Does a magnetic field exert a force on a charged object at rest, and does a charged object at rest produce a magnetic field?✗ Electric charges at rest interact magnetically: a magnetic field exerts a force on a charge at rest, and a charge at rest produces a magnetic field, just as it produces an electric field.   ✓ No.
Does the magnetic field of a long straight wire point directly away from (or toward) the wire?✗ The magnetic field of a current-carrying wire points directly away from the wire (or toward it), as the electric field of a charged line does.   ✓ No.
Is magnetic flux just another name for the magnetic field?✗ Magnetic flux is the same thing as the magnetic field strength, so the flux through a loop is set by B alone, whatever the loop's area.   ✓ No.
