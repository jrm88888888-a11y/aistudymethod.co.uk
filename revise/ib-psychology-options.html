Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

Are mental disorders simply a lack of willpower?✗ Yes, they are just a personal choice or weakness.   ✓ No — they have biological, cognitive and environmental causes.
Can psychological disorders be treated effectively?✗ No, disorders can never improve.   ✓ Yes — therapies like CBT and other treatments are effective.
What does the behaviourist approach study?✗ The unconscious mind and dreams.   ✓ Observable behaviour and how it is learned.
Do all psychological approaches explain behaviour the same way?✗ Yes, all approaches agree on causes.   ✓ No — they differ (e.g. behaviourist, cognitive, biological, psychodynamic).
