Key concepts

Periodic motionMotion that repeats itself in equal intervals of time.
Period, TThe time taken for one complete cycle of an oscillation.
Displacement from equilibrium, xThe position of an oscillating object measured from its equilibrium position, positive on one side and negative on the other.
Total mechanical energy of an oscillating system, E_totalThe sum of the system's kinetic and potential energies, E_total = U + K.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer. Make sure you know the right one:

Is every motion that repeats in equal time intervals simple harmonic motion?✗ Any motion that repeats itself in equal intervals of time is simple harmonic motion.   ✓ No.
Is x = A sin(2πft) the formula for every object in SHM?✗ The position of any object in SHM is x = A sin(2πft) (or A sin(ωt + φ)), whatever the object is doing at t = 0, and the phase constant is found with the sine form even when the cosine form is given.   ✓ No.
Do different points of a swinging rigid body oscillate with different periods, like simple pendulums of different lengths?✗ Each part of a physical pendulum swings like a separate simple pendulum of its own length, so points farther from the axis have longer periods.   ✓ No.
