Particle Model of Matter

States of matter, density and pressure, heating and the kinetic model — explaining the behaviour of solids, liquids and gases.

Particle Model of Matter is a core part of GCSE Physics (AQA, Edexcel, OCR, CCEA and IGCSE). Use the key concepts and common misconceptions below to revise, then lock them in with the free games.

Common misconceptions to avoid

✗ The energy required to raise the temperature of 1 kg by 1 degree. ✓ The energy required to change the state of 1 kg of a substance without changing its temperature.
✗ Kinetic energy doubles because energy is proportional to speed. ✓ It quadruples (because KE = 1/2 mv^2 — kinetic energy is proportional to v^2).
✗ It showed that atoms are solid spheres of positive charge with electrons embedded inside. ✓ Most of the atom is empty space, with a tiny dense positive nucleus.
✗ kg/m^2 ✓ kg/m^3
✗ rises ✓ constant
