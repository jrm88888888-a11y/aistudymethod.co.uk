Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer. Make sure you know the right one:

Is the current at an instant equal to the charge that has passed divided by the time elapsed?✗ The current at any instant is q/t, the total charge that has passed divided by the time elapsed, as in the relation for a constant current.   ✓ Not in general.
Does a resistor whose two ends are joined by an ideal wire still carry part of the current?✗ A resistor bypassed by a wire still carries part of the current, and still adds its resistance to the circuit.   ✓ No.
Does connecting more capacitors together always give a larger equivalent capacitance?✗ Capacitances simply add whatever the connection, because more capacitors can store more charge.   ✓ No.
