Number

Place value, fractions, decimals and percentages, primes and factors, powers, surds and standard form.

Number is a core part of GCSE Maths. Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

Place valueThe value of a digit depends on its position.
Integers & negativesWhole numbers including negatives; rules for + − × ÷.
Primes & factorsPrime numbers, factors, multiples, HCF and LCM.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What is the value of x^0 (for x not equal to zero)?✗ x^0 equals 0 because raising something to the zero gives nothing.   ✓ 1 — any non-zero number to the power of zero equals 1.
What does x^(1/2) mean?✗ x^(1/2) means x divided by 2 — half of x.   ✓ The square root of x (the positive root when x is positive).
Fully factorise 6x + 9.✗ 3(2x + 9), by only dividing the first term by the common factor.   ✓ 3(2x + 3) — take out the common factor 3.
Does multiplying always make a number bigger?✗ Yes, multiplication always increases a number.   ✓ No — multiplying by a number between 0 and 1 makes it smaller.
How do you decide the order of operations?✗ Always strictly left to right.   ✓ Brackets, indices, then ×/÷, then +/− (BIDMAS).
