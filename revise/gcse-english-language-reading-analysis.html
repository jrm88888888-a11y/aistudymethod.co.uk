Reading & Analysis

Analysing how writers use language and structure to influence the reader.

Reading & Analysis is a core part of GCSE English Language. Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

Multiple interpretationsOffering two valid readings of one quote
Conceptualised responseReading anchored to a big-picture idea
JuxtapositionPlacing opposites side by side for contrast
Structural shiftA clear change in focus, pace or tone
Cumulative effectImpact built up across multiple devices
Authorial intentWhat the writer is trying to achieve overall
Embedded quoteShort quote slotted naturally into your sentence
ZoomAnalysing one specific word in depth
Verb choiceHow the chosen action word shapes meaning
Adjective choiceHow descriptive words shape reader response
Effect on readerHow a technique makes the reader feel/think
Tentative languageHedging — 'perhaps', 'suggests', 'implies'
PEELPoint, Evidence, Explanation, Link — paragraph structure
PETERPoint, Evidence, Technique, Effect, Reader

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What is an apostrophe for?✗ Making a word plural.   ✓ Showing possession or omission (contractions).
What does analysing non-fiction involve?✗ Summarising what the text says.   ✓ Examining how language and structure achieve the writer's purpose.
