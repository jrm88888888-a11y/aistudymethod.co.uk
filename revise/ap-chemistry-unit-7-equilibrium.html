Unit 7: Equilibrium

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer. Make sure you know the right one:

Are the concentrations of reactants and products equal at equilibrium?✗ At equilibrium the amounts, concentrations or partial pressures of reactants and products are equal.   ✓ No.
When solving for an equilibrium concentration, can the K expression be used without the exponents?✗ The equilibrium expression is the ratio of product to reactant concentrations without exponents, so [X] = K_c × [X₂].   ✓ No.
Is the molar solubility of a salt the same in any solution, because its K_sp is constant?✗ Because K_sp is a constant at a given temperature, the molar solubility of a salt is the same whatever else is dissolved in the solution.   ✓ No.
