A-Level Psychology Revision

Issues & Debates

Free will vs determinism, nature vs nurture, reductionism, and gender and culture bias.

Issues & Debates is a core part of A-Level Psychology (AQA, Edexcel, OCR and other boards). Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

HolismBehaviour explained by whole system — Gestalt, humanistic.
ReductionismExplaining behaviour by simpler component parts.
Biological ReductionismReducing behaviour to genes, hormones, neurochemistry.
Environmental ReductionismReducing behaviour to stimulus-response chains.
IdiographicFocus on individuals (case studies, qualitative).
NomotheticFocus on laws applying to groups (experiments, surveys).
Hard DeterminismAll behaviour fully caused by internal/external forces.
Soft DeterminismBehaviour caused but allows some conscious choice (James).
Free WillWe choose our actions independent of biological/social causes.
NatureInnate, genetic, biological influences on behaviour.
NurtureEnvironmental and learned influences on behaviour.
Diathesis-StressInteraction: vulnerability triggered by stressor.
Alpha BiasExaggerating differences between men and women.
Beta BiasMinimising real differences between men and women.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

Do all psychological approaches explain behaviour the same way?✗ Yes, all approaches agree on causes.   ✓ No — they differ (e.g. behaviourist, cognitive, biological, psychodynamic).
What is the behaviourist view on the nature-nurture debate?✗ Both nature and nurture equally.   ✓ Strongly nurture - we are shaped by environmental conditioning.
