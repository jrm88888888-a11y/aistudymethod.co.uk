Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

Where does digestion begin?✗ In the stomach only.   ✓ In the mouth — mechanically by teeth and chemically by amylase in saliva.
What does homeostasis maintain?✗ Conditions that never vary at all.   ✓ Stable internal conditions within narrow limits, despite external change.
What does insulin do to blood glucose?✗ Raises blood glucose.   ✓ Lowers it, by making cells take up glucose.
How does water enter root hair cells?✗ By active transport using energy.   ✓ By osmosis — passively, down a water potential gradient.
How many circuits does human blood flow through per cycle?✗ One single loop around the body.   ✓ Two — a double circulation (heart→lungs→heart→body).
