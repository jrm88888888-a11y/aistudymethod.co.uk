Spectroscopy & Analysis

Mass spectrometry, infrared and NMR spectroscopy, and chromatography.

Spectroscopy & Analysis is a core part of A-Level Chemistry (AQA, Edexcel, OCR and other boards). Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

Mass spectrometryFinds relative masses and structure from mass/charge ratios.
Infrared spectroscopyIdentifies bonds and functional groups from absorption peaks.
NMR spectroscopyReveals carbon and hydrogen environments in a molecule.
ChromatographySeparates and identifies components by relative attraction.
FragmentationBreakdown of ions in mass spectrometry, giving structural clues.
Structure determinationCombining techniques to deduce a molecule's structure.
Molecular ion M+Rightmost peak = molecular mass.
M+1 peakFrom 13C isotope; ~1.1% per C.
M+2 peakIndicates Cl (3:1) or Br (1:1).
Base peakTallest peak; normalised to 100%.
TOFTime-of-flight mass spectrometer.
TMSTetramethylsilane reference; delta=0 ppm.
Chemical shift δPosition of NMR signal, ppm.
IntegrationPeak area ~ number of H atoms.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What does mass spectrometry measure?✗ The colour of a compound.   ✓ Mass-to-charge ratios, giving relative masses and structural clues.
What does infrared (IR) spectroscopy identify?✗ Exactly how much of a substance is present.   ✓ The bonds / functional groups present, from absorption peaks.
Do analytical instruments print a structure with no interpretation?✗ Yes, the machine directly states the structure.   ✓ No — spectra (NMR, IR, MS) must be interpreted to deduce structure.
What does a mole of a substance represent?✗ A mole is a unit of mass equal to the relative molecular mass in grams of any substance.   ✓ Avogadro's number (6.02 x 10^23) of particles of that substance.
If 50 cm^3 of a 1 mol/dm^3 solution is diluted to 100 cm^3, what is the new concentration?✗ The new concentration is 0.05 mol/dm^3 because you doubled the volume so you halve the concentration once and shift the decimal too.   ✓ 0.5 mol/dm^3 — the moles stay the same but the volume doubled.
