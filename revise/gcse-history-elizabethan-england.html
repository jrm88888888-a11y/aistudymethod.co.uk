Elizabethan England

Elizabeth I's reign — government, religion, the Spanish Armada and daily life.

Elizabethan England is a core part of GCSE History. Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

Northern Rebellion1569 Catholic earls' rising — easily crushed
Ridolfi Plot1571 plan for Spanish invasion and Mary as Queen
Throckmorton Plot1583 — French invasion plan, foiled by Walsingham
Babington Plot1586 — letters trapped Mary in a treason scheme
WalsinghamSpymaster whose agents broke the plots

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

Did Elizabeth I marry to secure her throne?✗ Yes, she married a foreign prince.   ✓ No — she never married, becoming the 'Virgin Queen'.
Why was the Spanish Armada (1588) defeated?✗ Only because English ships were bigger.   ✓ A combination of weather, fireships, English tactics and Spanish problems.
Was Elizabethan England free of religious tension?✗ Yes, everyone agreed on religion.   ✓ No — religion was a central issue; Elizabeth sought a 'middle way' but faced plots.
