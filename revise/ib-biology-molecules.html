Water & Biological Molecules

Water and its properties, and the structure and function of carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids.

Water & Biological Molecules is a core part of IB Biology. Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What happens to an enzyme after it catalyses a reaction?✗ It is used up and must be replaced each time.   ✓ Nothing — it is unchanged and can be reused many times.
What does heating an enzyme to a high temperature do?✗ It makes the enzyme work faster and faster with no limit.   ✓ It denatures the enzyme, changing the active site so it stops working.
