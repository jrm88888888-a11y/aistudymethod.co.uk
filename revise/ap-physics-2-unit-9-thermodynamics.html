Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer. Make sure you know the right one:

Does a gas push on its container because its atoms repel one another and push outward, even when at rest?✗ A gas presses on its container because its atoms repel one another and push outward, like compressed springs, whether or not they are moving.   ✓ No.
Is the internal energy of any object just the kinetic energy of its particles?✗ The internal energy of an object is only the kinetic energy of its particles, the quantity its temperature measures.   ✓ No.
Can a system's entropy change when no energy crosses its boundary?✗ A system's entropy can change only when energy is transferred into or out of it by heating or cooling; with no energy crossing its boundary, its entropy stays the same.   ✓ Yes.
