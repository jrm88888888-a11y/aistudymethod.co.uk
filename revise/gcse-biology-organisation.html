Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What drives water up the xylem in a tall tree?✗ Roots actively pump water up to the leaves like a heart.   ✓ Transpiration pull — evaporation from leaves creates tension that pulls water up the continuous water column.
How do hormones reach their target organs?✗ Hormones are sent through nerves directly to specific organs.   ✓ They are carried in the bloodstream and act on cells with specific receptors for that hormone.
What is the correct order of organisation?✗ Tissues → cells → organs → systems.   ✓ Cells → tissues → organs → organ systems.
Can a single cell be a complete living organism?✗ No, life always needs many cells.   ✓ Yes — many organisms (e.g. amoeba, bacteria) are single-celled.
