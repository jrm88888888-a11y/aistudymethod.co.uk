Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What does analysing a novel involve?✗ Summarising the plot of the story.   ✓ Examining how the writer uses character, structure, language and themes.
Is the narrator always honest and the same as the author?✗ Yes, the narrator always tells the truth and is the author.   ✓ No — narrators can be unreliable and are distinct from the author.
Is setting just where a story happens?✗ Yes, setting is only background detail.   ✓ No — it can build atmosphere, reflect themes and shape character.
