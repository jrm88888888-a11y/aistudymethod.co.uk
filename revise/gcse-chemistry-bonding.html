Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

Why does graphite conduct electricity but diamond does not?✗ Graphite conducts because it is a metal, while diamond is a non-metal.   ✓ Graphite has delocalised electrons between layers; diamond has all four outer electrons in localised covalent bonds.
When water boils, what is broken?✗ The covalent bonds between hydrogen and oxygen atoms are broken when water boils.   ✓ Intermolecular forces (hydrogen bonds) between water molecules are broken — the O-H covalent bonds stay intact.
Why do metals conduct electricity?✗ Metal atoms themselves move to carry the current.   ✓ Delocalised electrons are free to move through the lattice of positive ions, carrying charge.
Why does NaCl not conduct as a solid but does when molten?✗ ions are charged only when molten   ✓ ions are free to move when molten
Where is almost all the mass of an atom located?✗ Electrons contribute significantly to the mass of an atom.   ✓ In the nucleus, made up of protons and neutrons.
