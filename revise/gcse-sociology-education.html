GCSE Sociology Revision

Education

The role of education, factors affecting achievement, and sociological perspectives on schooling.

Education is a core part of GCSE Sociology. Revise the key concepts and common mistakes below, then lock them in with the free games.

Key concepts

SocialisationLearning the norms and values of society.
Hidden curriculumUnwritten lessons in punctuality, obedience, hierarchy.
Social solidarityA shared sense of belonging to society.
Role allocationSorting people into jobs that fit their ability.
MeritocracySystem where success is based on effort and talent.
Formal curriculumSubjects officially taught, like maths or English.
Correspondence principleBowles & Gintis: school mirrors workplace hierarchy.
Cultural capitalBourdieu: middle-class knowledge that helps in school.
Material deprivationLacking money for books, tuition or quiet study space.
LabellingTeachers giving pupils a fixed identity, e.g. 'bright'.
Self-fulfilling prophecyPupils live up — or down — to their label.
MarketisationTreating schools like a market with parental choice.
Meritocracy mythMarxist critique: school is rigged, not fair.
Gendered subjectsBoys and girls choosing different GCSE options.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

Can factors inside school affect achievement?✗ No, only home background matters.   ✓ Yes — labelling, setting and teacher expectations can affect results.
Does school success depend only on natural intelligence?✗ Yes, achievement is purely about ability.   ✓ No — class, gender, ethnicity and school factors all shape achievement.
