Key concepts

Dien Bien Phu 1954Decisive Viet Minh victory over French forces under Giap; ended French Indochina, led to Geneva Accords.
Geneva Accords 1954Agreement partitioning Vietnam at 17th parallel pending elections; US blocked elections to prevent Ho Chi Minh victory.
Gulf of Tonkin 1964Alleged North Vietnamese attack on USS Maddox; resolution gave Johnson power for full military involvement.
Tet Offensive 1968Viet Cong surprise attacks on 100 cities during lunar new year; military defeat but US public confidence collapsed.
My Lai Massacre 1968US troops under Lt. Calley killed 504 unarmed civilians; cover-up exposed 1969, devastated US moral authority.
Fall of Saigon 197530 April capture by North Vietnamese; helicopter evacuations from US embassy ended American involvement.

Common mistakes to avoid

Questions where students often pick the tempting wrong answer — make sure you know the right one:

What was the outcome of the Vietnam War?✗ The USA won a clear victory.   ✓ The USA withdrew and the North unified Vietnam in 1975.
Why did the USA struggle in Vietnam?✗ Because its army was tiny.   ✓ Guerrilla tactics, difficult terrain and limited local support made it unwinnable.
