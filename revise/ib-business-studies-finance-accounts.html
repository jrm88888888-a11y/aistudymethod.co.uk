Key concepts

Current ratioCurrent assets / current liabilities
LiquidityAbility to pay short-term debts
ROCEOperating profit / capital employed ×100
GearingLong-term debt / capital employed ×100
Capital employedEquity + long-term liabilities
Acid test(Current assets − stock) / current liabilities
Break-evenOutput where revenue = total cost
ContributionPrice minus variable cost per unit
Payback periodTime to recoup the initial outlay
ARRAvg annual profit / outlay ×100
NPVSum of discounted future cash flows
Discount rateRate used to bring future cash to today
RevenueTotal income from sales
Gross profitRevenue minus cost of sales
