AP® Physics Review

Free review and study guides for AP Physics 1, AP Physics 2, AP Physics C: Mechanics and AP Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism: unit reviews, AP-style practice questions and interactive lessons. Short, curiosity-driven lessons (about 20 minutes per mission) that teachers can assign. Use them alongside AP Classroom practice: they build the understanding behind the questions.

Choose your course. AP Physics 1 and 2 are algebra-based; the two AP Physics C courses use calculus. Each guide is organized by the College Board units.

AP Physics courses

AP Physics 1Algebra-based mechanics: kinematics, forces, energy, momentum, rotation, oscillations and fluids (Units 1–8).AP Physics 2Algebra-based thermodynamics, electricity and magnetism, optics, waves and modern physics (Units 9–15).AP Physics C: MechanicsCalculus-based mechanics: kinematics, dynamics, energy, momentum, rotation and oscillations (Units 1–7).AP Physics C: Electricity and MagnetismCalculus-based electricity and magnetism: fields, Gauss’s law, potential, capacitors, circuits and induction (Units 8–13).
