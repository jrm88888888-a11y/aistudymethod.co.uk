IB Chemistry Revision

Interactive topic lessons for the current IB Chemistry guide (first assessment 2025), organised by the Structure and Reactivity themes. Higher Level and Standard Level are separate — pick your level for each sub-topic.

Content follows the current Chemistry guide (first assessment 2025): two themes — Structure (the nature of matter) and Reactivity (how and why reactions occur). Most sub-topics have both SL and HL content; a few contain Additional Higher Level (AHL) material, and Reactivity 1.4 (Entropy and spontaneity) is HL only.
