About

The AI Study Method was created by two teachers — one in physics, one in languages — who wanted to give every student access to the kind of personalised, structured learning that was previously only available to those who could afford a premium tutor.

The Story

Why the
Velvet Method exists.

After years of teaching physics — from the classroom to one-on-one tutoring with individual students — a pattern became clear. The students who performed best weren't necessarily the most intelligent or the hardest working. They were the ones who studied strategically.

They knew what their syllabus required before they started. They identified their gaps early. They varied how they studied based on what worked for them. They tested themselves regularly rather than just re-reading. And they reflected on what they'd learned rather than simply moving on.

The problem was that nobody had ever taught most students these habits. They were the kind of meta-skills that were informally absorbed by some students — usually those with access to excellent tutors or highly educated parents — but never explicitly taught.

Then came AI. He saw its potential to put learning back in students' own hands — to give any student the kind of personalised, adaptive, patient support that was once reserved for those who could afford a premium tutor. The Velvet Method exists to make that potential real, and to help every student take genuine ownership of how they learn.

The Mission

Every student deserves a brilliant study partner.

Premium tutoring has always delivered better outcomes — but it's only ever been available to a minority of students. AI has the potential to change that. The AI Study Method exists to make sure every student knows how to unlock that potential.
