The Parent's Perspective

The question every
parent is asking.

Your child is using AI. So is every other student. The difference between a student who uses AI to copy answers and one who uses it to genuinely understand — and who performs better under exam conditions because of it — is whether they have a methodology.

The Velvet Method is that methodology. It doesn't just use AI — it teaches students how to use AI as a personalised tutor that adapts to their exact needs, identifies their specific gaps, and explains things in the way that works for them.

The result is a student who becomes genuinely independent — not dependent on tutors or on their phone screen, but on their own ability to learn effectively.
