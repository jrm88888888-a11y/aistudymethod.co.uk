Common Questions

Questions parents ask us.

How is the course delivered?
It's an online video course — short, on-demand lessons your child watches at their own pace, plus downloadable prompts and worked examples. It works on any device, any time, with lifetime access, so they can revisit it whenever they need it.
How much time does it take?
Around 45 minutes of bite-size video in total. Most students work through it over a few short sessions, then start applying the method to their real revision straight away.
What if my child has barely used AI before?
That's completely fine. The course assumes no prior experience and works with the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini — it teaches the how from scratch, safely and simply.
Will it work for my child's subjects and exam board?
Yes. The Velvet Method is a way of studying, not a set of notes, so it's subject- and board-agnostic. It works across GCSE, A-Level and the IB, for any subject and any exam board.
Does this replace a private tutor?
It's designed to reduce the need for one. The method teaches your child to get personalised, expert-level help from AI whenever they need it — building genuine independence rather than dependence on a tutor or a screen.
Doesn't using AI just encourage cheating?
The whole point of the Velvet Method is the opposite. It teaches students to use AI to understand, practise and test themselves — not to copy answers. The work and the thinking stay theirs.
What if it's not right for us?
Every course comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If it's not a fit, email us within 30 days and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.
