The Difference

This site gives you AI content.
This guide teaches you to create it.

The 160+ games, vocabulary tools, and roleplay stories on this site were all created using AI — primarily Claude and ChatGPT. The AI Study Guide shows you exactly how — so you can generate your own for any topic, any subject, at any level of difficulty.

A student who learns to use AI effectively as a study partner develops a transferable skill that serves them through GCSE, A-Level, university, and beyond.

Prompt Library — for Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI assistant

Prompts organised by
Velvet Method step.

V
Step 1
View the Bigger Picture
"Generate a complete topic map for AQA A-Level Biology showing how all topics connect and where the marks are concentrated."
"What are the most commonly tested command words in AQA A-Level Biology exam questions? Give me examples of what each demands."
"Based on this specification, create a personalised study priority list ranked by mark weighting."
E
Step 2
Evaluate Your Starting Point
"Ask me questions across all topics in AQA A-Level Biology to help me identify where my gaps are. At the end give me a priority list."
"Quiz me on [topic]. Probe deeper where I seem uncertain and move on when I'm confident. Then tell me what to focus on."
"Give me a diagnostic test across 10 topics from my specification. Mark my responses and identify my three weakest areas."
L
Step 3
Learn Creatively
"Create a memory match game testing vocabulary for the topic of Cell Division in A-Level Biology. Include 12 pairs."
"Write a roleplay scenario set in a hospital where I am a doctor and you are a patient — I need to apply my knowledge of the nervous system."
"Explain meiosis using a completely unexpected analogy. Then ask me to explain it back to you in my own words."
V
Step 4
Verify Your Knowledge
"Generate 8 exam-style questions on photosynthesis — a mix of 2-mark, 4-mark, and 6-mark questions. Mark my answers against a mark scheme."
"Give me a 20-minute timed challenge on [topic]. No hints. Then give me examiner feedback on my performance."
"Act as an AQA examiner. Ask me a 6-mark question on enzyme activity and mark my response strictly."
E
Step 5
Explore Application & Analysis
"Present me with an unseen data set on enzyme activity. Ask me to analyse it as an A-Level examiner would expect."
"Give me a real-world scenario that requires me to apply my knowledge of [topic]. Ask follow-up questions."
"Create a synoptic question that crosses the topics of cell biology, genetics, and ecology. Challenge me at A-Level standard."
T
Step 6
Transform Through Reflection
"Review our session. Which topics seem secure and which need another cycle? Give me a spaced repetition schedule."
"Which study methods did I use today? Which seemed most effective? How should I approach the next session differently?"
"Based on my performance today, create a two-week revision plan for my upcoming exam with specific daily targets."
